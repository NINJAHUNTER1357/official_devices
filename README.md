# Pixelify-AOSP: Official Devices

Welcome to the central repository for **Pixelify-AOSP's** official devices and Over-The-Air (OTA) configurations. This repository also serves as the application hub for developers looking to become official device maintainers.

---

## 🚀 Applying for Official Maintainership

We are always looking for dedicated developers to join our team! Before submitting your application, please ensure you review and meet all of our core guidelines.

### 📋 Prerequisites

Please read through the following documentation carefully. You must fulfill these requirements before submitting your application:

* **[Current Official Devices](docs/DeviceList.md):** Check the roster of currently supported devices.
* **[Device Stability Requirements](docs/device_requirements.md):** Ensure your builds meet our performance and quality standards.
* **[Maintainer Requirements](docs/maintainer_requirements.md):** Understand the expectations and ongoing responsibilities of a maintainer.
* **[Maintainers' Code of Conduct](docs/maintainers_code_of_conduct.md):** Review our community guidelines and behavioral expectations.

> **⚠️ Important Note on Selection**
> 
> Fulfilling the basic requirements does not automatically guarantee acceptance. Our team also evaluates applications based on previous development experience, technical proficiency, and your history of interactions within the community (respectful behavior towards users and fellow developers is a must).

---

## 📝 Submit Your Application

If your device meets our stability standards and you agree to follow our Code of Conduct and maintainer expectations, you are ready to apply!
